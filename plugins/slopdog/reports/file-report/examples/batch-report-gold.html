The Gmail pipeline: built, running, not yet joined up

Verdict. Every piece of the pipeline passes its tests and the Gmail bridge runs as a service on the ThinkPad, but the pieces are not connected to each other yet: the controller shows sample threads rather than your email, Slack is simulated, and the daily digest runs and sends nothing. Separately, the bridge stopped listening at 08:04 this morning after one network error, while its service still reports that it is running.

Why

You want workspace one to be Slack plus one communications dashboard instead of four apps. Gmail is the first of three feeders and builds the shared pieces the others reuse.

What

A service that hears each new email the moment it arrives, drafts one reply per thread as a real Gmail draft, shows it in a controller app, and pushes what matters to Slack, never sending without your approval.

How

Four layers in the comms repository (bridge, controller, brain, outputs), built behind fake Gmail and Slack stand-ins. LaForge's version won the A/B and now runs as systemd services.
