› '/home/broz/code/rockcap/data/backtest/candles' are these candles 100ms or 1 second i dont
  understand what i am looking at


• The backtest candle JSONs come from data/converted/by-day/*_100ms.csv (see
  BACKTEST_CANDLES_DIR in .env). However, the resulting candles have ~1,800 rows for a
  30‑minute window, which is 1 second granularity, not 100ms. That happens because the source
  trades timestamps in the shards are only second-level; when converted, every 100ms bucket
  collapses into the same second. So:

  - Source for candles: data/converted/by-day/{date}_100ms.csv → effectively 1s candles due to
    second-resolution timestamps.
  - Source for trades: data/shards/by-day/{date}.csv.

  If you need true 100ms candles, we’d need sub-second timestamps in the trades input;
  otherwise the converter can only produce 1-second buckets.
