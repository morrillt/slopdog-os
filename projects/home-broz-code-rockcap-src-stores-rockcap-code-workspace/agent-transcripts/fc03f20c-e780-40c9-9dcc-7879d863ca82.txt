## Expected Behavior
When navigating between days using Previous/Next buttons, the Cumulative Results section (day-by-day performance charts) should automatically update and display data for the current day. The section should populate without requiring any workaround like switching strategies.

## Observed Behavior
The Cumulative Results section (specifically the day-by-day performance charts on the right side) remains blank/empty when:
1. Initially loading a day (e.g., 1203)
2. Navigating to previous days (e.g., 1202, then previous again)
3. The section shows "Navigate days to collect data" or is empty

The workaround that fixes it:
- Switching to a different strategy (e.g., ATR strategy) and then switching back to the original strategy causes the section to populate correctly.

## Repro Steps
1. Navigate to localhost with default parameters:
   - PT: 20
   - SL: 10
   - Trigger: 8
   - Max trades: 2

4. Click "Previous" to go to day 1202

6. Click "Previous" again
7. Observe: Cumulative Results section is still blank
8. (Workaround) Switch to ATR strategy from dropdown, then switch back to original strategy
9. Observe: Cumulative Results section now populates correctly
